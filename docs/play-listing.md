## Full description

```
Paste a playlist link. Get music you'll actually like.

Music Man turns any Deezer or YouTube Music playlist into personalized
song and album recommendations — powered by real listening data, not ads
or algorithms trying to sell you something.

HOW IT WORKS
• Paste a playlist link from Deezer or YouTube Music (500+ tracks supported)
• Pick how many songs (30–150) and albums (5–30) you want
• Get ranked recommendations with plain-English explanations of WHY each
  pick fits your taste
• Fine-tune with 28 genre chips and 7 mood chips, or hit "Surprise me"

DISCOVER MORE
• Daily picks based on your favorites
• For You news feed: music headlines matched to artists you like
• Outbound links: open any song or album in Spotify, Deezer, YouTube,
  or YouTube Music

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No accounts. No servers. No ads. No tracking.
• Everything lives on your device — favorites, history, settings.
• Export your data as JSON anytime.

Built by an indie developer who just wanted better recommendations.
```
