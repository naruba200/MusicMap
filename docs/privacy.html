Privacy Policy — Music Man

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Music Man ("the app") is a music recommendation app that runs entirely on your device. There are no user accounts, no backend servers, and no analytics or advertising SDKs. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Data stored on your device

The app stores the following only on your device (never transmitted anywhere):

  • Playlists you import (track titles and artists) and the recommendations generated from them.
  • Your favorites (songs and albums), recommendation history, app settings, and cached music-news articles.

You can delete this data at any time from Settings, or by clearing the app's storage / uninstalling the app.

Network requests

To do its job, the app contacts these public services when you use the relevant features — only at your request:

  • Deezer public API — to load playlists you paste in.
  • YouTube — to load YouTube Music playlists you paste in.
  • Last.fm API — to compute music recommendations (anonymous metadata queries: artist/track names only).
  • Music-news RSS feeds (e.g. Pitchfork, NME, JROCK NEWS) — to fetch headlines for the News tab.

These requests contain no personal identifiers from the app. Each service's own privacy policy governs what it receives (e.g. your IP address).

What we do NOT collect

  • No accounts, emails, names, or contact details.
  • No location data.
  • No usage analytics or crash reporting.
  • No advertising identifiers.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with third parties for marketing.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and knowingly collects nothing from anyone.
